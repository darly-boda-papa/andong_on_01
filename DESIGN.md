## Brand & Style

The design system expresses the dignified gravity and poetic restraint of Korean cultural heritage, reimagined for a contemporary digital experience. Known as the spiritual capital of Korea, Andong’s identity is anchored in Confucian scholarship, natural landscapes, and enduring architectural traditions.

The aesthetic philosophy draws inspiration from **Hanok** (traditional timber and tile architecture) and **Hanji** (mulberry paper):
- **Restraint and White Space (여백의 미)**: Large, intentional negative space balances information-dense travel itineraries with a contemplative, unhurried pace.
- **Architectural Symmetry & Structural Lines**: Thin, deliberate hairline borders reference timber framing and paper door lattices (Changhoji).
- **Tactile Modernity**: Warm, paper-like surfaces replace stark digital whites, while saturated mineral pigments highlight interactive focal points without visual clutter.

Target users include international travelers, cultural heritage enthusiasts, and domestic visitors seeking architectural tours, Hahoe mask dance festivals, and traditional culinary experiences. The interface evokes quiet prestige, scholarly serenity, and effortless mobile clarity.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in classical Korean mineral and nature pigments:

- **Primary (`#1A446C` - Nakdong Indigo)**: Derived from the winding waters of the Nakdong River surrounding Hahoe Village. Used for primary actions, active navigation indicators, key icons, and high-emphasis brand elements.
- **Secondary (`#D24A43` - Dancheong Vermilion)**: An authentic red-clay accent drawn from traditional temple beam pigmentation. Reserved for cultural designation badges, live event markers, booking callouts, and urgent highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#1E232A` - Giwa Slate)**: A deep, muted char-black inspired by kiln-fired Hanok roof tiles. Serves as the primary typographic color and structural boundary tone.
- **Neutral (`#F8F6F0` - Hanji Ivory)**: A soft, warm paper ground that reduces eye strain and establishes an organic tactile presence across all base views.

### Supporting Functional Tones
- **Surface Elevation Light (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white layered selectively on cards and sheets to rise gently against the Hanji canvas.
- **Muted Stone (`#E4DFD3`)**: Low-contrast outline and divider tone reflecting raw granite foundations.
- **Secondary Text (`#5C626C`)**: Soft charcoal for secondary metadata, operational hours, and historical dates.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements a high-distinction editorial pairings model:

1. **Cultural & Curatorial Display (`Noto Serif`)**: Applied selectively to historical site names (e.g., 도산서원, 하회마을), exhibition hero banners, and deep cultural essays. The classical serif structure grounds the experience in authentic historical authority.
2. **System & Information Architecture (`Noto Sans`)**: Applied across interactive controls, body passages, data tables, map cards, and schedules. Provides neutral legibility on small viewports and pairs seamlessly with Korean and Latin scripts.

### Typographic Rules
- Headings set in `Noto Serif` maintain tighter negative letter spacing (`-0.02em`) to echo traditional woodblock ink-press printing.
- Body text maintains a comfortable 1.6x line-height ratio to facilitate fluid reading of multi-paragraph historic background summaries.
- Numerals in ticket prices, dates, and times adopt proportional, tabular alignments where comparative scanning is required.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is optimized for high-density mobile scanning with a baseline width of **390px** (iPhone standard width). 

### Layout Model
- **Grid Architecture**: 4-column fluid mobile grid with 20px (`1.25rem`) exterior screen margins and 12px (`0.75rem`) internal column gutters.
- **Horizontal Bleed Rails**: Heritage photography showcases and multi-card itineraries utilize edge-to-edge overflow carousels with an inset snap point aligned with the 20px screen margin.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm**: Standard 8pt base unit. Major narrative sections receive `space-xl` (36px / `2.25rem`) vertical spacing to evoke spatial breathing room (여백).
- **Responsive Adaptations**:
  - **Phone (<600px)**: 4 columns, single-column stacked card presentations, fixed sticky bottom navigation.
  - **Tablet (600px–1024px)**: 8 columns, 32px canvas margins, two-column split cards for itineraries and audio guide transcripts.
  - **Desktop (>1024px)**: Max container width constrained to 1080px to preserve editorial balance.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons deep, artificial drop shadows in favor of **structural layering and tactile paper strata**:

- **Ground Level (Canvas)**: Background surface in `#F8F6F0` (Hanji Ivory).
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks)**: Surface `#FFFFFF` bordered by a 1px crisp outline in `#E4DFD3`. Ambient elevation is achieved via an ultra-subtle, warm-tinted shadow: `0px 2px 8px rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Action Buttons & Popovers)**: Surface `#FFFFFF` with `0px 8px 24px rgba(26, 68, 108, 0.08)` and a 1px border of `#E4DFD3`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & Dialogs)**: Overlaid with a 40% opacity scrim in `#1E232A`. Sheet containers possess a distinct top border and a soft ambient vertical plume `0px -8px 32px rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.12)`.
- **Glass Accents (Navigation & App Bar)**: Semi-translucent `#F8F6F0` with 85% opacity and a `16px` backdrop-filter blur, anchored by a bottom hairline divider in `#E4DFD3`.

## Shapes

The shape system employs moderate, calculated corner radii (`roundedness: 2`) referencing the gentle curve of eaves in Hanok roofs (배흘림 and 처마선):

- **Default Element Radius**: `0.5rem` (8px) for buttons, text input fields, and pill chips.
- **Structural Containers (`rounded-lg`)**: `1rem` (16px) for standard content cards, heritage showcase items, and image preview modules.
- **Modals & Bottom Sheets (`rounded-xl`)**: `1.5rem` (24px) for upper corners of draggable presentation sheets and feature dialogs.
- **Badges & Tags**: Full pill roundness (`9999px`) to maintain clear differentiation from geometric content cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Deep Nakdong Indigo (`#1A446C`) solid fill, white text, 8px radius, 48px height. Active states shift to a deeper ink shade (`#133352`) with a subtle 0.98x spring scale.
- **Secondary / Heritage Button**: Hanji Ivory fill with a 1.5px solid border in Giwa Slate (`#1E232A`), slate text, 48px height.
- **Accent Button (Festivals/Alerts)**: Dancheong Vermilion (`#D24A43`) solid fill with white text for immediate registration or emergency travel updates.

### Chips & Badges
- **Cultural Designation Chips**: Capsule-shaped pill with 1px border in `#D24A43`, soft translucent vermilion fill (`rgba(210, 74, 67, 0.08)`), and Dancheong vermilion text (`label-sm`).
- **Filter Chips**: Pill shape, 32px height. Unselected state has `#FFFFFF` fill with 1px `#E4DFD3` border. Selected state transitions to solid Giwa Slate (`#1E232A`) fill with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Cards
- **Tour & Heritage Cards**: 16px corner radius, `#FFFFFF` background, wrapped in a 1px `#E4DFD3` outline. Imagery features an aspect ratio of 16:10 with an inner subtle vignette. Cultural tags sit pinned to the top-left with an 8px inset.
- **Audio Tour Mini-Card**: Horizontal layout with a 64px square thumbnail, 12px radius, serif title, duration badge, and a circular indigo play button.

### Lists
- Separated by 1px solid dividers using `#E4DFD3`. Left-aligned iconography in muted slate (`#5C626C`), primary labels in `title-md`, and subtle chevron indicators on interactive rows.

### Inputs & Form Elements
- **Input Fields**: 48px container, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E4DFD3` border, 8px radius. Active focus switches to a 1.5px border in `#1A446C` with zero shadow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 20px footprint, custom check glyph. Active fill uses `#1A446C` with a crisp white tick mark.

### Bottom Navigation Bar (390px Mobile Viewport)
- **Bar Anatomy**: Fixed at 64px height plus device safe-area inset. Backed by frosted Hanji Ivory (`rgba(248, 246, 240, 0.92)`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- **Top Border**: 1px hairline border in `#E4DFD3`.
- **Items**: 4 core destinations (Explore [탐색], Heritage Map [유산 지도], Audio Guide [오디오], Saved [보관함]). Active state highlights in `#1A446C` with a 3px rounded dot beneath the icon; inactive state sits in `#5C626C`.
